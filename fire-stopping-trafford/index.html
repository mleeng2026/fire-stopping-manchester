Fire stopping contractors in Trafford

Trafford has a different building mix from many Greater Manchester boroughs. The biggest difference is Trafford Park, where warehouses, manufacturing premises, logistics buildings and larger commercial units can contain extensive mechanical and electrical services running through compartment walls and floors.

That can involve large cable trays, grouped electrical services, pipework, ventilation, plant rooms, risers, structural steel and existing fire stopping disturbed by later alterations.

Move towards Stretford, Old Trafford, Sale, Urmston and Altrincham and the mix changes again to offices, retail, healthcare, residential developments and commercial refurbishments.

We are FIRAS-approved passive fire protection contractors working on both new installations and remedial projects across Trafford.

Fire stopping in Trafford Park

Trafford Park has a very different fire stopping requirement from a small office or residential property. A single industrial building may contain warehouse space, production areas, offices, storage, plant rooms, electrical rooms, mezzanines and service corridors.

Typical project work

Electrical upgrades, mechanical installations, warehouse alterations, factory changes, new cable containment and compartmentation survey remedials.

Tell us the programme

If contractors are already working on site, tell us where you are in the programme. That can make a big difference to how the fire stopping is planned.

Large cable trays & service penetrations

Industrial and commercial fire stopping can involve much larger openings than a simple single-cable penetration. One opening might contain several cable trays, electrical bundles, data cabling, pipework, insulated services or several different services together.

The installed system needs to suit the wall or floor, the opening size, the services passing through it and the required fire resistance. It is not a case of simply filling the visible gap.

Electrical upgrades

Electrical contractors often create new openings through existing compartment walls. If fire stopping is left until ceilings and containment are fully installed, access can become much harder.

Pipe penetration sealing

Different pipe materials can require different systems. Combustible pipework may require a tested collar or wrap, while metal pipework and insulated services can need another detail.

Fire stopping during warehouse & factory alterations

Industrial buildings change over time. A warehouse gains new offices. Machinery moves. An electrical system is upgraded. A new tenant changes the layout. Additional pipework or data cabling is installed.

Every change can create another penetration through the building's compartmentation. During refurbishment we may find services pushed through existing fire stopping, open gaps around cables, damaged fire batt, unsuitable foam or filler, penetrations hidden above ceilings, or old services removed but the opening left behind.

Where defects appear throughout the building, it usually makes more sense to tackle them as one remedial programme rather than dozens of disconnected repairs.

Has a fire stopping survey found defects?

Send us the full report or defect schedule. If your survey contains 50 defects, we need to understand all 50 rather than price from one photograph.

Survey remedials need completing • handover is approaching • electricians have created new penetrations • ceiling contractors are waiting • existing seals have been damaged • refurbishment has uncovered old defects • structural steel needs fire protection.

Tell us the actual problem. That is more useful than trying to make the enquiry sound technical.

Intumescent paint contractors in Trafford

We provide intumescent paint application for structural steel throughout Trafford. This is particularly relevant to warehouses, industrial buildings, commercial developments and refurbishment projects where steel needs a specified period of fire resistance.

For a quotation, send structural drawings, the steel schedule, section sizes, required fire resistance, coating specification, photographs and programme dates.

Fire stopping for fit-outs & refurbishment projects

Commercial fit-outs can create a lot of new penetrations very quickly. Electrical, data, plumbing and ventilation contractors may all be running services through the building at the same time.

Leaving passive fire protection until the final few days can create access problems and hold up handover. We work alongside main contractors and M&E teams on office fit-outs, warehouse offices, retail work, tenant alterations and larger building upgrades.
