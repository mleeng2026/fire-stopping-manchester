Buyer guide

What matters before fire protection work starts

Open the answer that matches your situation. Each section explains the decision, the information that helps and the quickest useful next step without making drawings or specifications a barrier to enquiry.

Which of the two main services does your project need?

Choose fire stopping services when cables, pipes, ducts or other services pass through a fire-rated wall or floor, or when a compartmentation survey has identified openings and defective seals. This includes service penetrations, cable trays, pipe and duct openings, fire batt, mastic, collars, wraps and survey-led remedial work.

Choose intumescent paint application to structural steel when beams, columns or connections need a specified period of structural fire protection. The steel scope is assessed separately because section sizes, exposure, primer, environment, required resistance and finish can all change the coating build-up.

What can you send for a fire stopping quotation?

Start with the Manchester or Greater Manchester site location and a plain explanation of the live problem. Useful information can include wider and close photographs, rough quantities, a defect schedule, marked-up drawings, the fire strategy detail, steel schedules or a coating specification. Send what already exists; a complete technical pack is not required to make first contact.

Explain what is driving the enquiry: a ceiling is about to close, an inspection has raised non-conformities, handover is approaching, a tenant area must reopen, or structural steel is waiting for follow-on trades. That allows the urgent decision to be understood before less important paperwork is requested.

What changes when the building is occupied or the work is hard to reach?

Occupied apartment blocks, offices, schools, healthcare settings, hotels, restaurants, theatres, warehouses and industrial units cannot all be programmed in the same way. Tell us about residents or tenants, security, inductions, permits, restricted hours, locked risers, ceiling access, working height, stored goods and live operations. These details affect how work is grouped and accessed, even when the penetration detail itself is similar.

Mechanical and electrical trades also need to be considered. Sealing too early can lead to completed fire stopping being disturbed by another cable or pipe; sealing too late can leave openings hidden above ceilings or hold up inspection. A useful sequence identifies completed routes, routes that may still change and the date each area becomes inaccessible.

How do 60, 90 and 120-minute fire resistance requirements work?

The required period normally comes from the building’s fire strategy, design or project specification. It is not a rating that can be added to any product or opening by description alone. A 60, 90 or 120-minute requirement must be supported by a tested or assessed system that covers the actual wall or floor, service arrangement, opening, installation detail and required integrity or insulation performance.

For structural steel, the required period is only one input. The steel section factor, number of exposed sides, environment, primer and selected intumescent system can change the required dry film thickness. Send the requirement if you have it; if not, make the enquiry and tell us which design information is still outstanding.

Do you handle fire-retardant spray treatment for decorative materials?

Yes, where the material, project requirement and suitable treatment can be confirmed. A recent Manchester nightclub and restaurant project involved extensive artificial planting across two floors after the council required fire-retardant treatment. That is a real example of a decorative feature creating a practical fire-safety requirement in a live hospitality fit-out.

See our fire retardant treatment Manchester service for venue and commercial interiors. Artificial foliage, fabrics, timber and other decorative materials are not automatically covered by one product or one performance claim. The substrate, treatment system, application rate and required evidence need checking. We will not describe a treatment as “60-minute” or give another rating unless the supporting specification, test or classification genuinely says so.
