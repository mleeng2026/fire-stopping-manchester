Intumescent paint applicators for Manchester steelwork

Our intumescent paint applicators work on beams, columns, connections & steel frames for commercial developments, industrial units, refurbishments & exposed architectural steel. For principal contractors, steelwork contractors & quantity surveyors, the first task is to define the steel scope and the required protection before access is lost to walls, ceilings or services.

The right specification depends on the steel sections and the fire protection period required by the design. That is why we price from a steel schedule, drawings or a specification rather than guessing — so the coating that goes on matches what the building actually needs.

Structural steel fireproofing is not priced by floor area alone. The required fire resistance, section factor, exposure, primer, environment and finish all influence the specified build-up and dry film thickness.

Steel information

Section sizes, lengths, exposure on two, three or four sides and connection details help define the quantity and thickness.

Preparation and compatibility

The steel condition and existing primer or coating must be identified before the intumescent system is selected or applied.

Programme and protection

Access, weather, curing time and the risk of damage from follow-on trades need to be planned into the coating sequence.
