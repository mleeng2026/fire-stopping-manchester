Fire stopping contractors in Salford

Fire stopping work across Salford varies considerably depending on the building. Around Salford Quays, MediaCity and Ordsall, projects are often apartment blocks, offices, mixed-use developments, communal areas, service risers and commercial fit-outs.

Across Eccles, Pendleton, Swinton, Walkden and Worsley, the building mix changes again: schools, healthcare premises, older commercial property, residential developments and buildings that have been altered several times over the years.

The actual problem is often straightforward. An electrician has run new cables through a compartment wall. New pipework has been installed. A ceiling has been opened during refurbishment. Or a compartmentation survey has found defects that now need closing out.

We are FIRAS-approved passive fire protection contractors carrying out new installation and remedial fire stopping throughout Salford and Greater Manchester.

Has your fire compartmentation survey found defects?

A survey report is one of the most common reasons clients contact us. You might have five defects in one plant room or a much larger schedule covering several floors.

Typical defects

Open service penetrations, damaged fire batt, gaps around cables and pipework, ceiling-void defects, openings in risers and old seals that have been disturbed.

What to send

Send the full report or defect schedule where possible, plus the site postcode, drawings, photographs, quantities and your programme or handover date.

Where the information is clear, we can start assessing the work from the report, drawings and photographs. Where access or construction details are unclear, a site visit may be the better option. Send what you have and we can tell you what is missing.

Apartment blocks & mixed-use buildings

The amount of residential and mixed-use development around Salford Quays, MediaCity and Ordsall creates a particular type of fire stopping work.

Defects are often hidden in service risers, communal corridors, ceiling voids, electrical cupboards, basement service areas, plant rooms and penetrations between separate fire compartments.

A new cable or pipe may only need a small hole, but if it passes through a fire-rated wall or floor that opening needs to be properly reinstated. If the building is occupied, tell us when you enquire so access and phasing can be planned sensibly.

Cable, pipe & service penetration fire stopping

Modern buildings contain a huge amount of electrical and mechanical infrastructure. Those services regularly pass through walls and floors that form part of the building's compartmentation.

Electrical cables & cable trays

Electrical upgrades, fibre routes and data services can create penetrations through several compartment walls. If contractors are still on site, it is often better to deal with the fire stopping before ceilings and boxing are closed.

Cable tray fire stopping

Pipe penetrations

Plastic and metal pipework can require different treatments depending on pipe diameter, insulation and wall or floor construction. Send photographs and measurements if you are unsure.

Pipe penetration fire stopping

Depending on the opening, the installation may involve fire batt, fire-resistant sealant, collars, pipe wraps, cavity barriers or another tested passive fire protection system. We do not treat every penetration as if it needs the same product.

Fire stopping during refurbishment work

Refurbishments regularly uncover problems that have been hidden for years. Once ceiling tiles, wall linings or service boxing come down, you can suddenly find old penetrations, damaged seals, unsuitable foam or services passing through compartments without proper protection.

The refurbishment itself can create more openings for electrics, plumbing, ventilation and data. If another trade is waiting for the fire stopping before they can close a ceiling or wall, tell us the deadline.

“We have around 20 penetrations above the corridor ceilings. The electricians are finished and the ceilings need closing next week.”That is useful information. You do not need to write a technical essay.

Commercial fire stopping around Salford Quays & MediaCity

Fire Stopping Salford FAQs

Can you carry out work from a fire compartmentation survey?

Yes. Send the complete survey or defect schedule and we can review the defects and the information available for pricing.

Can you work in an occupied apartment block?
