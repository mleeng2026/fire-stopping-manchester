Fire stopping contractors in Salford

Fire stopping work across Salford varies considerably depending on the building. Around Salford Quays, MediaCity and Ordsall, we are more likely to be dealing with apartment blocks, offices, mixed-use developments, communal areas, service risers and commercial fit-outs.

Across Eccles, Pendleton, Swinton, Walkden and Worsley, projects can also include schools, healthcare buildings, older commercial property, residential developments and buildings that have been altered several times over the years.

The actual problem is often straightforward: an electrician has run new cables through a compartment wall, new pipework has been installed, a ceiling has been opened during refurbishment, or a compartmentation survey has found defects that need closing out. We are FIRAS-approved passive fire protection contractors carrying out new installation and remedial fire stopping throughout Salford and Greater Manchester.

Has your fire compartmentation survey found defects?

A survey report is one of the most common reasons clients contact us. You might have five defects in one plant room or a much larger schedule covering several floors.

  • Open service penetrations
  • Damaged or incomplete fire batt
  • Gaps around cables and pipework
  • Defects above suspended ceilings
  • Openings in service risers
  • Damaged or unsuitable old seals

Send us the full survey or defect schedule where possible. That gives us a much better picture than one photograph taken from the report. Where the information is clear, we can start looking at the work from the report, drawings and photographs. Where access or construction details are unclear, a site visit may be the better option.

What should I send for a quote?

Site postcode, survey or defect schedule, drawings, photographs, approximate quantities, required fire resistance if known, and your programme or handover date. You do not need everything before contacting us. Send what you have and we can tell you what is missing.

Apartment blocks & mixed-use buildings

The amount of residential and mixed-use development around Salford Quays, MediaCity and Ordsall creates a particular type of fire stopping work. Defects are often hidden in service risers, communal corridors, ceiling voids, electrical cupboards, basement service areas and plant rooms.

A cable or pipe may only require a small hole, but if it passes through a fire-rated wall or floor it needs to be properly reinstated. In an occupied building, access may also need coordinating with residents, building management, tenants and other contractors.

If the building is occupied, tell us when you enquire so we can plan around it.

Cable, pipe & service penetration fire stopping

Modern buildings contain a huge amount of electrical and mechanical infrastructure. Those services regularly pass through walls and floors forming part of the building's compartmentation.

Depending on the opening, the installation may involve fire batt, fire-resistant sealant, collars, pipe wraps, penetration sealing systems, cavity barriers or other tested passive fire protection systems. The right detail depends on the construction and what is passing through it. We do not treat every penetration as if it needs the same product.

Electrical cables & cable trays

Electrical upgrades, fibre routes and data services can create penetrations through several compartment walls. If contractors are still on site, it is often better to deal with the fire stopping before ceilings and boxing are closed.

Pipe penetrations

Plastic and metal pipework can require different treatments depending on pipe diameter, insulation and wall or floor construction. Send photographs and measurements if you are unsure.

Fire stopping during refurbishment work

Refurbishments regularly uncover problems that have been hidden for years. Once ceiling tiles, wall linings and service boxing come down, you can suddenly find old penetrations, damaged seals, unsuitable foam or filler and services passing through compartments without proper protection.

The refurbishment itself can then create more penetrations for electrics, plumbing, ventilation and data. If another trade is waiting for the fire stopping before they can close a ceiling or wall, tell us the deadline.

“We have around 20 penetrations above the corridor ceilings. The electricians are finished and the ceilings need closing next week.”
That is useful information. You do not need to write a technical essay.

Intumescent paint contractors in Salford

We also provide intumescent paint and structural steel fire protection across Salford. Intumescent coatings are used where structural steel needs to achieve a specified period of fire resistance.

The coating thickness depends on the steel sections, section factors, required fire resistance and tested coating system being used. For a quotation, send structural drawings, the steel schedule, section sizes, required fire resistance, photographs and programme dates.

We provide airless spray application and other suitable methods where appropriate to the specification.

Commercial fire stopping around Salford Quays & MediaCity

Buildings around Salford Quays and MediaCity can contain substantial electrical, communications and mechanical infrastructure. One new service route may pass through several fire compartments before reaching its destination.

For example, a fibre or electrical route could start in a basement service area, pass through risers and continue onto upper floors.
