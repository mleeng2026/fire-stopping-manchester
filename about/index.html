Passive fire protection is all we do

Fire Stopping Manchester MT is a specialist passive fire protection contractor. Our focus is fire stopping — sealing the service penetrations, gaps and compartment breaches that fire and smoke would otherwise pass through — along with intumescent coatings for structural steel.

We work for main contractors, facilities and property managers, landlords and building owners across Manchester and Greater Manchester. Whether it is a new-build programme, a fit-out, or remedial works flagged by a survey or fire risk assessment, we get the compartment lines sealed and the paperwork signed off cleanly.

Because this is a specialist discipline, the detail matters. Fire stopping only performs if it is installed the way the system was fire tested. That is the standard we work to on every penetration.

How we work

Sealed to the tested system

Fire stopping only works if it is installed the way it was tested. We use the correct products and methods for each penetration and substrate — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Priced from real information

We quote from drawings, remedial schedules, surveys or photos — so the price reflects the actual job, and you know what you are getting.

Built around your programme

Much of our work is in live, occupied and active commercial sites. We plan works around access windows, follow-on trades and handover dates.

What we cover

  • Service penetration fire stopping — cables, pipes and ducts
  • Cable tray and cable transit fire stopping
  • Pipe and duct penetration sealing
  • Compartmentation surveys and remedial works
  • Fire batt, mastic, collars and wraps
  • Intumescent paint for structural steel
