Compartmentation & Remedial Works Manchester

A fire compartmentation survey or fire risk assessment has landed you a list of defects — open penetrations, breached walls, missing fire stopping. We turn that schedule into completed, closed-out works, so you can evidence compliance instead of managing an open action list.

From defect list to closed out

Compartmentation defects rarely come one at a time. A survey typically returns a schedule of items across risers, corridors, plant rooms and voids — penetrations, gaps at wall heads, poorly formed fire stopping and missing seals. Managing that list while the building stays occupied is the hard part.

We are set up for exactly this: pricing against a remedial schedule, working through it methodically, and giving you the records to prove each item is done. That is what turns a survey action into a signed-off, evidenced piece of work.

What we close out

Remedial schedules from fire compartmentation surveys
Non-conformities and defects from fire risk assessments
Open penetrations, breached walls and gaps at wall heads
Poorly-formed or failed existing fire stopping
Missing seals around services in risers and corridors
Handover close-out lists on new-build and refurbishment

How we work

1

Price the schedule

Send the remedial schedule or survey report and we price the works item by item, or as a package.

2

Methodical close-out

We work through the schedule with tested systems installed to the manufacturer’s specification, phased around occupancy.

3

Minimise disruption

In occupied buildings we plan access and phasing to keep disruption to residents, staff or tenants down.

4

Evidence each item

Photographic records of completed items are available on request to close out the schedule and support the fire safety file.
