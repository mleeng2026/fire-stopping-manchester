Fire stopping across Stockport

Stockport town centre has been through major regeneration, with new residential, office and mixed-use buildings alongside the refurbishment of existing stock. New-build and refurbishment both rely on compliant fire stopping around every service that crosses a compartment.

We work with contractors and building owners across Stockport on service penetration sealing, cable tray and pipe fire stopping, and compartmentation remedial programmes — including occupied buildings where the works have to be phased carefully.
