Fire stopping for Urmston refurbishments & fit-outs

Urmston has a different building mix from Trafford Park. Around the town centre and the A56 corridor there are offices, shops, hospitality premises, apartments, healthcare and managed buildings, while Flixton, Urmston Moor and Davyhulme bring more residential and mixed-use property into the picture. Refurbishment and fit-out work can expose older service routes above ceilings, inside risers and behind boxing. New electrical, data, plumbing or ventilation work can then create further penetrations through fire-resisting walls and floors.

We deal with the practical end of that problem: identifying the openings in scope and installing an appropriate tested fire stopping system for the service and substrate. That can include fire batt and mastic, pipe collars or wraps, linear seals, cavity barriers and other passive fire protection systems where suitable.

If electricians, ceiling installers or another contractor is waiting, tell us your programme date when you enquire. It helps us understand the urgency immediately.

Survey remedial works

Pipe penetration fire stopping

Fire batt & mastic

Fire batt & mastic

Compartmentation defects found above ceilings or in risers

A lot of remedial work only becomes obvious once someone looks above a suspended ceiling, opens a service riser or removes old boxing. The issue might be a cable route that was never sealed, an old fire batt that has been cut for new services, pipework passing through an oversized opening, or a previous seal that no longer matches what now passes through it.

Where a survey has already identified and numbered the defects, send the full schedule rather than individual screenshots if possible. It gives us the context, quantities and locations. For live refurbishment work, photographs and marked-up drawings are particularly useful before areas are closed.

We do not assume every hole needs the same product. The service, substrate, opening size, existing construction and required fire performance all matter.

That means access can matter as much as the number of defects. Intumescent paint for structural steel

We also provide intumescent paint and structural steel fire protection for Urmston projects. This is separate from penetration sealing but often appears on the same refurbishment or construction programme.

For a useful quotation, send the structural drawings or steel schedule where available, section sizes, required fire resistance, coating specification if one has been set, photographs and programme dates. What happens when you ask for a quote?

Send the site postcode and what you know. If you have a compartmentation survey, fire strategy, marked-up drawings or defect schedule, attach them. If you do not, send photographs and approximate dimensions. Tell us whether the site is live, whether ceilings or walls are waiting to close, and any handover or inspection date.

Fire Stopping Urmston FAQs

Do you carry out fire stopping in Urmston?

Yes. We cover Urmston, Flixton, Davyhulme, Urmston Moor, Carrington and surrounding Trafford areas for commercial, residential and refurbishment projects.

Can you quote from a compartmentation survey?

Yes. Send the complete survey or defect schedule, site postcode and any drawings or photographs you have. We can review the defects and tell you if anything else is needed.

Can you help if we do not have a survey?

Yes. Send clear photographs, dimensions, the location of the penetrations and a description of the work. Depending on the scope, we can advise what further information is required.

Can you fire stop cable trays and electrical penetrations?

Yes. We carry out fire stopping around cables, cable trays and other electrical services using systems appropriate to the opening, substrate and required fire resistance.

Do you carry out remedial fire stopping before handover?

Can you work in an occupied commercial or apartment building?

Potentially, yes. Tell us about occupants, access times and any phasing restrictions so the work can be assessed properly.
